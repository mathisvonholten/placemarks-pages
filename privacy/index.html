Privacy Policy for Placemarks

Placemarks does not collect or sell personal data from the app. The app does not include advertising, tracking, or developer-operated analytics.

App data

The app does not use a developer-operated account system, advertising, analytics, tracking, or a developer server.

Saved pins, place names, coordinates, favorites, visited status, and notes are stored on your device. When iCloud sync is available, this saved-place data also syncs through your private iCloud database.

Apple operates iCloud and CloudKit. Placemarks’ developer does not receive or inspect records in your private iCloud database.

Recent searches and app settings remain on your device and do not sync through iCloud. Placemarks does not receive or access this data.

If you use Export Data, Placemarks creates the export on your device and passes it to the system share sheet so you can choose where to save or send it. Exported files can include your pins, notes, visited status, and favorite status. Placemarks does not upload exported files itself; a destination you choose handles the file according to its own privacy practices.

Depending on your device settings, Apple may include app data in device backups. Placemarks does not control or access those backups.

Share Map

Share Map is optional. When you create a shared map, Placemarks creates a read-only snapshot of the pins you choose. The snapshot can include pin names, map locations, and visited or favorite status. Notes are not included, and the shared map does not contain a sender name or profile.

Shared map snapshots are encrypted on your device and stored using Apple’s CloudKit. The information needed to decrypt and open a snapshot is contained in the share link, so anyone with the complete link can view it while the link remains active. Recipients can open a shared map in the Placemarks App Clip or the installed app.

The sharing feature does not send the decryption information to Placemarks’ developer, and the developer does not inspect shared map contents. Your active shared links are managed through your private iCloud database so they remain available across your devices.

Shared map links stop working after 30 days and can be revoked earlier. Placemarks attempts to remove the associated encrypted snapshot after a link is revoked or expires. Because storage is provided by Apple’s CloudKit, physical deletion may not happen immediately.

Apple Maps

Placemarks uses Apple Maps and MapKit to search for and display places. When you search for a place, select a map feature, or view map content, Apple may receive the information needed to handle that request, such as search text, place information, coordinates, and map region information. Those Apple Maps and MapKit requests are handled by Apple according to Apple’s privacy practices. The same applies when a shared map is viewed in the App Clip.

Placemarks does not request access to your current location.

Support

If you contact support, the information you choose to include in your message is used only to respond to your request. Support messages may include your email address, device details, iOS version, and anything else you choose to send.

Support email is handled through Apple’s iCloud Mail service.
